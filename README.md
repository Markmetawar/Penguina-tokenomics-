# Penguina-tokenomics-

The total supply of Penguina follows a fair-launch distribution model with full transparency and no hidden allocations.

100% of the liquidity pool was permanently burned, ensuring that liquidity is locked and cannot be removed or manipulated by any deployer or team wallet. This establishes long-term trust and removes liquidity-related risk.

There is no pre-allocated or hidden team supply. Following the community takeover (CTO), the current team operates using tokens acquired from the open market and their own holdings. These holdings are voluntarily used for future marketing, development, and ecosystem growth initiatives.

All tokens are part of the circulating supply and are publicly tradable through decentralized exchanges. There are no additional mint functions or hidden supply mechanisms.

Overall, Penguina is a fully community-driven project with permanently burned liquidity and a transparent distribution model where marketing efforts are funded through openly held, self-acquired tokens.
